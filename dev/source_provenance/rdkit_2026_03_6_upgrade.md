## Method and integration

Review starts from the upstream source diff and follows each changed function's helpers, callers, data tables and error paths. Implementing functions carry verbatim source anchors and separate behavior/complexity markers under [the source-reproduction protocol](../source_reproduction_protocol.md). Fixed regressions belong at their smallest stable owner boundary; prepared corpus and designated special-regression comparisons belong to the standard parity runner. Expectations are not relaxed or regenerated to fit COSMolKit.

The upgrade preserves COSMolKit's approved public names, generated operation contracts, block-level COW and failure atomicity. Public tautomer queries and value transforms do not write caches back to the caller; source-required working-cache updates stay detached.
